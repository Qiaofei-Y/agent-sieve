P0Setup ~half a day

Stand up both collection layers. Confirm each machine is running before moving on.

sudo zkg install zeek/ja4
sudo zeek -i eth0 -C sieve-s2/zeek/sieve-ja4.zeek
# Confirm ssl.log has the ja4 field and conn.log has orig_bytes
P1Build the baseline ~2 weeks

Observe only, do not alert — let the system learn what "normal" looks like on your network. Do not skip this step: skip it and the whole network reads as false positives during validation.

P2Run the T1–T7 self-test ~1 day

From one LAN machine, send labeled test traffic and record the time window of each run. After each test, click on the right to record whether S3 caught it.

P4Evaluate ~10 minutes 
python3 sieve-verify/evaluate.py --from-live \
  --pihole-db /etc/pihole/pihole-FTL.db \
  --zeek-logs /opt/zeek/logs/current \
  --labels labels.json --window 60
P5Tune and re-test as needed

If you fall short, tune one thing per the attribution and re-run once — don't change a pile at once, or you won't know which change did what.

P6Draw the conclusion
The criterion: precision is the line between life and death. Only when you can reliably keep false positives low (high precision) will anyone use it; recall can be filled in gradually later. Raising recall at the cost of raising false positives too is a step backward.

Progress and numbers are saved locally in this browser. Switching devices or clearing the cache loses them.
